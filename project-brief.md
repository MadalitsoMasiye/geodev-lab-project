# My project brief
## The question
Which areas of Nsanje are prone to flooding activities based on elevation and location from water bodies? 
## The data I need
District boundaries https://www.masdap.mw
Digital elevation model https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov
Waterways https://www.masdap.mw
Rainfall and flood data https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps
Land use and land cover https://esa-worldcover.org/en
## What I will build
I intend on building a dashboard used for land use planning that will flag areas that are prone to heavy flooding for residential, agricultural and industrial purposes
